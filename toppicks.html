Welcome to My Top Picks

Spider-Man: No Way Home is one of my favourite superhero movies. It has amazing characters, exciting action scenes and emotional moments. Here are my personal top picks from the movie.

My Top 5 Favourite Characters

1. Peter Parker (Spider-Man)

Peter Parker is the main character of the movie. He is brave, caring and always tries to protect the people he loves. His journey makes the movie exciting and emotional.

2. Doctor Strange

Doctor Strange is a powerful sorcerer who helps Peter Parker. His magical spell accidentally creates a multiverse problem.

3. Green Goblin

Green Goblin is one of the most dangerous villains in the movie. His personality and actions make him a memorable enemy for Spider-Man.

4. MJ

MJ is Peter Parker's girlfriend. She supports Peter during difficult situations and plays an important role in his life.

5. Ned Leeds

Ned is Peter's best friend. He brings humour and friendship to the story and helps Peter during his adventure.

Best Movie Moments

These are some of the most exciting and memorable moments from Spider-Man: No Way Home.

  • Doctor Strange's spell goes wrong.
  • The return of villains from previous Spider-Man movies.
  • All three Spider-Men meet for the first time.
  • Spider-Man faces Green Goblin.
  • The final battle to save the multiverse.
  • The emotional ending of the movie.
